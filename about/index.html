The Outprix Standard

Our 4 Core Operating Principles

We don’t believe in bloated agency retainers or vanity metrics. Here is the operational philosophy behind every growth system we engineer.

Principle 01 🎯

Radical Attribution Truth

Zero vanity metrics. Every rupee accounted for.

We never report on "impressions" or "engagement rate" as primary success metrics. We track server-side conversions, qualified sales calls, blended ROAS, and net pipeline value. If ad spend doesn't create compounding business equity, we pivot immediately.

Principle 02 ⚡

7-Day Sprint Velocity

High-velocity creative and angle iteration.

Ad algorithms favor freshness and direct-response momentum. We work in disciplined 7-day sprint cycles—testing fresh hooks, landing page variants, and audience clusters weekly so your campaigns never suffer creative fatigue or stagnation.

Principle 03 🤝

Direct Founder Accountability

Direct access to Bajrang, not junior interns.

You will never be pitched by a senior partner only to have your account handed off to an entry-level trainee. At Outprix, founder Bajrang Choudhary personally architect your growth strategy, reviews ad performance daily, and maintains direct communication.

Principle 04 🌐

Hybrid AI & Search Authority

Future-proofed for the LLM search revolution.

We bridge traditional search engine optimization with next-generation Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). We ensure your brand entity is authoritative enough that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI explicitly recommend your brand when prospects ask buying queries.
