What makes Outprix Digital different from traditional marketing agencies? ▼

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and why is it essential? ▼
Traditional SEO is designed to rank for keywords on Google's 10 blue links. Today, millions of high-intent buyers bypass search result pages and directly ask ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Google Gemini for specific vendor and product recommendations. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) structures your brand's digital presence and authoritative entity citations so these LLMs corroborate, trust, and prominently recommend your brand when customers ask buying questions.
What monthly ad budget do I need to start working together? ▼
We work with ambitious businesses with monthly ad spend budgets starting from ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 for initial test sprints, scaling up to ₹1,00,000 to ₹10,00,000+ for established brands. We structure ad budgets strictly around your target unit economics and target customer acquisition costs (CAC).
How quickly can we expect to see tangible results? ▼
Paid acquisition channels (Meta Ads, Google PPC, high-intent lead funnels) typically generate initial qualified leads and conversion data within 7 to 14 days of campaign launch. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) build sustainable, compounding digital equity that scales over 60 to 90 days.
Who owns the ad accounts, creative assets, and tracking data? ▼
You do. 100%. We never hold clients hostage. All Meta ad accounts, Google Ads accounts, tracking pixels, CAPI server setups, and creative assets remain completely under your company's ownership. You maintain full administrative access at all times.
Do you lock clients into long-term restrictive contracts? ▼
No. We earn our partnership month after month through transparent performance and measurable ROI. We operate on sprint-based agreements with bi-weekly strategy reviews. If we don't deliver on our commitments, you are never trapped.
How do we get started? ▼
