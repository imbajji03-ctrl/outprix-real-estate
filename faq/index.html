Frequently Asked Questions

Honest data on ad budgets, lead quality filters, Jaipur real estate market CPLs, and campaign execution.

1. Why should real estate developers hire a specialized real estate agency?

Real estate is not e-commerce. You cannot sell a ₹75 Lakh apartment through a simple "Shop Now" button. It involves high ticket sizes, multiple family decision-makers, and long buying cycles. Generalist agencies generate cheap junk clicks that waste your sales team's time. Outprix Real Estate builds multi-step verification funnels that qualify buyers by budget, location preference, and purchase timeline before passing the lead to you.

2. What is the realistic Cost Per Lead (CPL) for real estate Meta Ads in Jaipur?

In Jaipur's current market:

  • Affordable Housing (₹25L – ₹45L): Qualified CPL typically ranges from ₹120 to ₹200.
  • Mid-Segment Flats & Plots (₹50L – ₹1.2 Cr): Qualified CPL ranges from ₹200 to ₹400.
  • Luxury Villas & Penthouses (₹2 Cr+): CPL ranges from ₹600 to ₹1,200 due to rigorous income and occupation filtering.

3. How do you prevent fake or unresponsive leads?

We use a 3-layer anti-junk defense:

  • Custom Lead Forms: We avoid pre-filled single-tap forms. Users must manually answer key qualifying questions.
  • OTP & WhatsApp Verification: The user receives an automated WhatsApp brochure immediately to confirm number validity.
  • Live CRM Sync: Leads are delivered in real time to your sales team with lead source tracking.

4. How long does it take for campaigns to start generating leads?

From the moment we finalize your project assets and USP, onboarding and campaign setup take 5 to 7 business days. Once launched, inquiries typically begin arriving within 24 to 48 hours of ad delivery.

5. Do you handle creative production, video walkthroughs, and ad copies?

Yes. We handle end-to-end creative production including high-converting video hooks, drone footage scripting, property walkthrough reels, 3D render animations, and direct-response ad copy in Hindi and English tailored for Jaipur buyers.

6. What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for real estate?

GEO is the process of structuring your project's digital presence so AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite and recommend your residential or commercial project when high-net-worth buyers search conversational queries like "best luxury villas in Jaipur with RERA approval."
